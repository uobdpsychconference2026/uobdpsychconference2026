Who's in the Driver's Seat: Exploring Self-Knowledge in the Age of Tech

We are in an age of something unprecedented and almost paradoxical; our algorithms seem to know us better than we know ourselves. They have mapped our patterns, know what we like, anticipate our needs and wants, and actively shape what appears on our screens (often without us noticing).

Now, I am not an Tech Scientist or AI researcher. However, I am interested in how AI and technology are impacting us in terms of our development and well-being. I've seen clients and students rattle off sophisticated concepts in psychology and mental health. However, when it comes to understanding or experiencing that concept, there is a gap. However, when it comes to collecting information vs understanding a concept, there is a gap. Now, educational institutions try to help us move from knowing to understanding for major content areas, but there really isn't a curriculum for understanding yourself. After all, we are in the "information age" (ironically, not the "knowledge age").

This talk will explore ways to deepen and interact with our own self-knowledge and self-awareness. Using emotional intelligence as a framework, we will explore what emotional intelligence is, how it helps us build self-knowledge and self-awareness as we engage with technology. While we may fantasize about life before smartphones, social media and AI, we are past the point of turning back. And so, the idea is to help us feel a deeper sense of self-fortification so that we can attempt to maintain our spot in the driver's seat when driving these tech highways.

Speaker bio

Dr. Chasity O'Connell is a Professor of Practice at American University in Sharjah, where she teaches psychology courses and conducts research on emotional intelligence, clinical supervision, social media, FOMO, and psychological well-being. She is also Clinical Director at Thrive Centre for Wellbeing in Dubai, where she provides therapy and clinical supervision, and has developed and currently supervises their innovative Early Career Professional Program to support the next generation of therapists in the UAE. With extensive experience across clinical practice, education, and international mental health initiatives, Dr. O'Connell is committed to advancing therapy education, therapeutic services, and mental health advocacy in the UAE.
